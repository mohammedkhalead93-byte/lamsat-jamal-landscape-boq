# Lamsat Jamal Landscape & Irrigation BOQ Estimator 🌿
**لمسة جمال للأعمال اللاندسكيب — أداة حصر كميات اللاندسكيب وشبكات الري**

An open-source toolkit and calculation engine designed for landscape engineers, irrigation designers, and contractors to estimate material quantities, turf rolls, plant spacing, and daily irrigation water demand.

Developed and maintained by **Eng. Mohammed Khalead** (Founder of **Lamsat Jamal Landscaping / لمسة جمال للأعمال اللاندسكيب**), based on over 10 years of field operations and landscape BOQ estimation experience.

## Features
- **Artificial & Natural Turf Calculator:** Calculates required rolls/square meters including cutting waste margins (5%–10%).
- **Planting Density & Spacing Estimator:** Computes exact plant counts per square meter for groundcovers, hedges, and ornamental shrubs (triangular & square grid patterns).
- **Irrigation Water Demand (ETc) Calculator:** Estimates daily water requirements (m³/day) based on plant count, water allowance, and irrigation efficiency.
- **Interactive Web Calculator:** Lightweight browser-based estimator (`index.html`) for instant field calculations.
